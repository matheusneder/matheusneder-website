About

How I got here

My full name is Matheus Villela Neder Issa, though I go by Matheus Neder professionally. I have a degree in Computer Engineering from PUC Minas in Brazil and started working in 2003 as a freelance web developer and Linux systems administrator. Since then, I've worked in product development, software engineering and solutions architecture, much of it at banks, dealing with their security, reliability and governance requirements.

At those banks, I designed architectures for moving mainframe systems to the cloud while keeping existing services running. At Olé Consignado, I also helped introduce one of the first on-premises Kubernetes clusters in Belo Horizonte and redesign commission services to use asynchronous messaging with Apache Kafka.

My work involves decisions about performance, growth, integration and cost, as well as the day-to-day problems of keeping systems running. I've led technical teams, mentored developers and worked with colleagues across disciplines and locations.

Music

I also play guitar. In 2011, I started posting my instrumental tracks online. English singer and lyricist Johnny Gray wrote lyrics and vocal melodies for them, and we began recording together as Neder & Gray.

We recorded remotely, sending files back and forth over the internet. I recorded guitars and bass in Belo Horizonte; Johnny recorded vocals in Leeds, England. The music draws on 1970s rock and hard rock, with plenty of guitar distortion. My guitar influences include Gary Moore, Steve Vai, Joe Satriani and Zakk Wylde.
